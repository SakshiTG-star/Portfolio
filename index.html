Driver Drowsiness Detection System

Real-time driver fatigue monitoring system engineered for high accessibility and affordability.

Project Overview & Objective

Drowsy driving is a leading cause of highway accidents globally. This project was developed to create a cost-effective, real-time safety module capable of detecting driver fatigue through optical cues and generating instant acoustic warnings before critical incidents occur.

Key Features & System Architecture

  • Facial Landmark Tracking: Continuously calculates Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) and Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) to identify microsleep and prolonged yawning.
  • Real-Time Alarm Triggers: Emits low-latency alerts when drowsiness thresholds are exceeded across consecutive frames.
  • Low-Cost Edge Optimization: Engineered to run smoothly on standard webcams and affordable edge compute processors without expensive proprietary sensors.

Technologies & Tools Used

Python OpenCV Computer Vision NumPy / SciPy Embedded Hardware

Smart Waste Segregation System

Automated multi-category waste sorting unit leveraging inductive, proximity, and moisture sensors.

Project Background & Leadership (SIH 2026)

Developed during the internal Smart India Hackathon 2026 where I led an interdisciplinary team of 6 engineers. The project tackled inefficient municipal waste management by automating the separation of wet organic refuse, dry waste, and metallic scrap at the disposal point.

Hardware & Sensor Workflow

  • Multi-Sensor Fusion: Inductive sensors detect metallic objects, capacitive moisture probes classify biodegradable wet waste, and IR proximity sensors detect item arrival.
  • Automated Actuation: Microcontroller coordinates servo-driven mechanical flaps that direct classified waste into segregated containment bins.
  • Real-Time Fill Telemetry: Ultrasonic level sensors track container capacity and signal when disposal is required.

Technologies & Hardware Stack

Embedded C / Arduino Microcontrollers Inductive & Moisture Sensors Servo Actuators Team Leadership
